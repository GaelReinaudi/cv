\begin{rubric}{Summary}
\noentry{2001--2005}
\text{{\parskip=0pt \parindent=0pt
Film professional and programmer with twenty years of work on the circulation of French,
Francophone and international cinema between France and the United States. Former US
Representative for Unifrance (2013--2024), overseeing the Rendez-Vous with French Cinema
festival at Film at Lincoln Center; guest programmer at Metrograph; co-founder of Uptown Film
Center and Uptown Flicks; consultant for the CNC and Cin\'e Tamaris.
\par
Studied at ENS Ulm, Sciences Po Paris and Freie Universit\"at Berlin, with theses on the
Deutsches Theater in East Berlin and on Berlin in German cinema. Lead researcher for Serge
Toubiana's \textit{L'Amie am\'ericaine} (2020), a book on Truffaut's collaborator Helen Scott,
and co-author of Unifrance's \textit{25 ans de cin\'ema fran\c{c}ais \`a l'\'etranger}.
\par
Current interests focus on women and filmmakers from minority and diaspora communities, both
through the rediscovery of overlooked film figures (Liliane de Kermadec, Tahar Cheriaa, Khady
Sylla, Anja Breien) and through the emergence of new generations of French and Francophone
talent working at the crossroads of several cultures. Other interests include the history of
creative and economic exchanges between Francophone and American filmmakers and its recent
evolution.}}
\end{rubric}
