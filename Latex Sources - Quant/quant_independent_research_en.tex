\entry*[]%
\href{https://github.com/gQuantCoder/deep_orderbook}{\texttt{deep\_orderbook}}:
research platform for market microstructure signal discovery---built on the conviction that
order book dynamics contain far richer information than price-derived indicators.
\begin{cvitems}
  \item Live exchange connection (Coinbase); event-driven asyncio pipeline converting book/trades live data
  into spatially and temporally correlated rolling images for deep vision models.
  \item Supports online model training with real-time visual display of predictions,
  giving the researcher immediate intuition about how a strategy reacts and trades as it learns.
  \href{https://youtu.be/HOB_fD5DuW8}{\texttt{youtu.be/HOB\_fD5DuW8}}
  \item Hermes\slash OpenClaw: research loop (hypothesis generation, experiment execution, evaluation) automated via agentic harness.
\end{cvitems}
