\begin{rubric}{Selected Architecture Work}
  \noentry{2008}

  \subrubric{Trust-Building AI for Safety-Critical Operations}

  \entry*[]%
  Built a voice-driven aviation operational assistant combining structured event capture, situational
  state, procedural and regulatory retrieval, and decision support while preserving human authority
  over consequential actions.

  \entry*[]%
  Used the same application logic in a no-consequence simulator and historical-event replay, letting
  users experience how the AI actually behaves, discover its strengths and limits for themselves, and
  gradually build trust before relying on it in consequential situations.
\end{rubric}
